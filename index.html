Highlands Coffee eLearning Programs

Role

eLearning Specialist

Project Overview

Developed and continuously improved digital learning programs to support employee onboarding and capability development across Highlands Coffee. Contributed to the organization's transition toward more scalable and engaging digital learning experiences.

The Challenge

When eLearning initiatives were first introduced, many learners were unfamiliar with self-paced digital learning. Key challenges included:

  • Learners had limited experience navigating eLearning courses
  • Overly complex interactions could create confusion and reduce completion rates
  • Learning paths needed to be delivered within tight monthly timelines
  • The learning team had to balance speed, usability, engagement, and instructional effectiveness
  • As the number of learning programs increased, development efficiency became increasingly important

My Solution

I adopted a phased approach that gradually increased learning sophistication while ensuring learners remained comfortable with the platform and learning format.

Phase 1: Establish Learner Familiarity

  • Designed simple and intuitive courses focused on ease of use
  • Included clear navigation guidance and learning instructions
  • Prioritized accessibility and user-friendly interactions
  • Gathered feedback on learner behavior and engagement

Phase 2: Enhance Learning Engagement

  • Introduced a wider variety of interactions and learning activities
  • Incorporated scenario-based questions, knowledge checks, and interactive elements
  • Improved visual storytelling and learner participation
  • Increased engagement while maintaining usability

Phase 3: Improve Development Efficiency

  • Leveraged AI tools to accelerate content drafting, ideation, and production workflows
  • Developed reusable templates and standardized development processes
  • Reduced production effort for recurring learning components
  • Maintained instructional quality while meeting aggressive development timelines

Impact

  • Helped learners successfully adapt to eLearning as a learning channel
  • Increased the sophistication and engagement level of learning experiences over time
  • Supported the delivery of approximately one learning path per month
  • Improved development efficiency through AI-assisted workflows and reusable assets
  • Balanced learner experience, instructional quality, and business deadlines

Tools Used

Articulate Storyline 360, ChatGPT, Gemini, Capcut, Vyond, Instructional Design, Learning Experience Design

TTC AgriS Sugarcane Harvester elearning Course

Role

Project Lead, Instructional Designer & eLearning Developer

Project Overview

Led the development of a digital training program for agricultural machinery operation and maintenance by converting highly technical English manuals into engaging eLearning experiences for operational staff.

The Challenge

The project faced multiple constraints:

  • A large volume of technical documentation needed to be converted into learning content
  • The content was highly specialized and required close collaboration with subject matter experts
  • The project timeline was extremely tight, with only three weeks allocated for script development
  • Video production resources were limited
  • Filming plans changed frequently due to field conditions and operational constraints
  • Subject matter experts were not always available for recording sessions

My Solution

As the primary project executor, I managed the entire production workflow from content analysis to final course delivery. I:

  • Translated and analyzed technical source materials
  • Converted complex manuals into learner-friendly instructional content
  • Wrote eLearning scripts and learning scenarios
  • Developed detailed storyboards
  • Created shooting plans and managed production schedules
  • Coordinated on-site recording activities in Tây Ninh
  • Adjusted filming plans in real time when circumstances changed
  • Reorganized production workflows overnight to keep the project on schedule
  • Managed all source files and production assets
  • Prepared expert interview questions and documented technical clarifications
  • Edited videos and integrated multimedia assets into the final eLearning course
  • When expert voice recording became unavailable, proposed and implemented an approved AI voice solution to maintain the project timeline

Impact

  • Successfully delivered the project within the required deadline
  • Converted highly technical engineering content into accessible digital learning experiences
  • Maintained project quality despite changing requirements and resource limitations
  • Demonstrated leadership in coordinating content development, multimedia production, stakeholder communication, and course development simultaneously
  • Produced a complete end-to-end training solution, from source materials to final learning delivery

Tools Used

Articulate Storyline 360, Camtasia, Video Production, Storyboarding, Instructional Design, Project Coordination

UNICEF Parenting Project

Role

eLearning Developer & Learning Design Support

Project Overview

Supported the development of a large-scale parenting eLearning program involving multiple subject matter experts, content contributors, and developers. The project required strong coordination and standardized development practices to ensure consistency across learning modules.

The Challenge

The project involved numerous stakeholders and contributors, with content being developed by multiple experts and team members. Key challenges included:

  • Maintaining consistency across courses created by different developers
  • Ensuring design standards were applied uniformly throughout the program
  • Managing extensive client feedback and revision cycles over several months
  • Preventing conflicts between newly requested changes and previously approved requirements
  • Supporting efficient collaboration across a distributed development team

My Solution

To improve consistency and streamline development, I focused on creating scalable development standards and quality control processes. I:

  • Developed storyboard support for selected learning modules
  • Designed reusable course templates that could be easily adopted by other developers
  • Established visual and interaction standards to maintain a consistent learner experience
  • Created detailed development checklists to support quality assurance and reduce errors
  • Managed and tracked client feedback throughout multiple review cycles
  • Verified that requested revisions were implemented accurately while maintaining alignment with previous approvals
  • Supported the team in applying standardized development practices across the project

Impact

  • Improved consistency across courses developed by multiple team members
  • Reduced development errors through structured quality control processes
  • Simplified onboarding and collaboration for developers working on the project
  • Helped manage a lengthy review process while maintaining project quality and alignment with client expectations
  • Contributed to the successful delivery of a large-scale UNICEF learning initiative

Tools Used

Articulate Storyline 360, Storyboarding, Template Design, Quality Assurance, eLearning Development, Stakeholder Coordination
